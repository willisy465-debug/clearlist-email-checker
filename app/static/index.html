    Format, length, and reserved domains that can never accept mail.

  2. 02 DNS and MX

    Connect, EHLO, MAIL FROM, RCPT TO, then quit. The message is never sent.

    Known disposable inboxes, typos, role accounts, catch-all servers, and trap-style names.
